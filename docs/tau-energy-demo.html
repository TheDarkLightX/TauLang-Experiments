License and Authority Boundary

This website is intentionally small in what it publishes. The public page does not carry Tau source, Tau binaries, or the full formula corpus. The replay path regenerates local evidence after the reader reviews Tau's license.

Hard Rules

  • TauEnergy can rank route order, but cannot accept a route result.
  • WES can schedule checks, but cannot accept without a checker.
  • Tau or deterministic route certificates decide validity.
  • Local replay is required before treating a performance claim as current.
